# Item-level review final

Survey Review and Installation Review now support review inside one shop at evidence level.

- Select one measurement/photo or multiple items.
- Approve Selected persists item-level approval.
- Redo Selected creates correction tasks only for selected evidence and assigns them to the same surveyor/installer.
- Other accepted evidence remains untouched.
- Each row visibly shows APPROVED or REDO.
- Final whole-survey / whole-installation approval is blocked until every required measurement/work item and photo has explicit APPROVED status.
- Migration 0085 adds `field_review_decisions` for durable review state/auditability.
