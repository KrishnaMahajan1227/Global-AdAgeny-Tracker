The old way of doing OOH

Sound familiar?

Most dealer branding rollouts in India still run on WhatsApp groups, Excel sheets and someone's best guess. That's where budgets leak and trust breaks.

No visibility

You find out about delays after they've already cost you a launch window

Status updates arrive as forwarded photos in a WhatsApp thread, days after the shop was supposedly done — if at all.

Billing disputes

Invoices don't match what actually got installed

Without a live tally between the PO and the field, "billed" and "installed" quietly drift apart — and someone has to reconcile it manually, every month.

Fraud risk

You can't always tell a real installation from a recycled photo

One installer photo, no GPS, no angle variety, no way to catch a duplicate — and you're paying for boards that may not exist.

Rate leaks to the field

Commercial terms end up visible to people who shouldn't see them

Rate cards travel down WhatsApp chains to surveyors and installers, weakening your negotiating position on the next PO.

No single source of truth

Every stakeholder has a different version of "where things stand"

Your trade marketing team, the agency owner and the installer on the ground are all working off different spreadsheets.

One-size pipeline

Not every PO needs survey, design, production and install

Sometimes you just need supply, or just an install, or just a dealer onboarded — but the agency still runs it through the same rigid process.

How Darshan works

One pipeline. Every stage tracked. Nothing skipped.

From the moment a shop is surveyed to the moment it's billed, every stage is logged, gated and visible — to your team, and to you.

01

Survey

Field teams capture site photos, mark exact board dimensions and log GPS — checked live against your PO budget as they go.

02

Design Approval

Every design is reviewed side by side with the marked survey photo, so what's approved actually fits the wall it's going on.

03

Production

Nothing enters the queue until every material and component checklist is ready — no half-built boards leaving the workshop.

04

Load Check

Before a vehicle leaves for site, what's loaded is checked against what was ordered — a step most agencies skip entirely.

05

Installation

GPS-tagged, multi-angle before/after photos, logged on site — fraud-proofed against duplicate or reused images.

06

Billing

Invoices are raised against what was actually installed and reconciled live against the PO — not a separate, disconnected record.
